## Explicit non-claims

Publication of v0.6.0 does **not** establish any of the following:

- physical NVIDIA GPU, CUDA, NCCL or real vLLM acceptance;
- physical Huawei Ascend, CANN or real vLLM-Ascend acceptance;
- a real non-Kind Kubernetes production-cluster acceptance (`E1_NOT_RUN`);
- production deployment, HA/SLA certification, or production control-plane throughput;
- full public Alibaba production-trace replay.

Those claims remain blocked until the corresponding real-environment plan is executed and its evidence is bound to an exact commit.
